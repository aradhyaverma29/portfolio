AI resume analyzer

Most resumes get filtered out by ATS software before a person reads them. ResumeXray helps fix that. It analyzes a resume for ATS compatibility, matches it to a job role, and points out missing keywords and skill gaps.

I led the UI/UX design and frontend development, creating a responsive interface where users upload a resume and get personalized, real-time suggestions, with reusable components throughout.
